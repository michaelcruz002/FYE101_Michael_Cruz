# My Fall 2026 Courses

## 🎮 CSC383-A — Game Engines

![Game Engines](images/csc383-game-engines.jpg)

**Game Engines** is a computer science course that connects programming with game development. Game engines bring together different systems that are needed to make a game work, including gameplay, graphics, physics, input, and other interactive features.

I am interested in this course because I enjoy games and want to understand more about what happens behind the scenes when a game is created.

## ☢️ SYN392-A — The Nuclear Age

![The Nuclear Age](images/syn392-nuclear-age.jpg)

**The Nuclear Age** looks at the development and impact of nuclear technology during the modern era. The course gives me an opportunity to learn more about how nuclear weapons, science, politics, and society have affected the world.

I am interested in understanding not only the technology itself, but also the historical events and decisions that shaped the Nuclear Age.

## 😴 PSY306-A — Sleep Psychology

![Sleep Psychology](images/psy306-sleep-psychology.jpg)

**Sleep Psychology** focuses on sleep and how it connects to psychology and everyday life. The course gives me a chance to learn more about topics such as sleep habits, the effects of sleep on the mind and body, and why healthy sleep is important.

This is a course I find interesting because sleep is an important part of everyday life, especially for students balancing school and everything else.
